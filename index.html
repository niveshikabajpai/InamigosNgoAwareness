WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

Founded in 2020, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization working towards creating meaningful social impact across India.

Its work focuses on important causes including education, community support, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and skill development.

Through volunteers and dedicated initiatives, InAmigos aims to create a more inclusive, compassionate and empowered society.
